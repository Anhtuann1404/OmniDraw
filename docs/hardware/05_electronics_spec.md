## 1. Purpose

The Electronics and Control subsystem provides the motion processing, power regulation, sensor interfacing, and actuator drive circuitry for OmniDraw Plotter V1. It executes G-code commands generated by the software pipeline, driving the CoreXY planar steppers and the Z-lifter servo while monitoring safety endstops and limit positions.

## 3. Candidate Hardware Components

All component selections in this section represent candidate architectures and remain subject to formal review and verification during the electronics integration phase.

### 3.1 Motion Controller Mainboard
- **Candidate Architecture:** 32-bit ESP32-based CNC motion board (e.g. Makerbase MKS DLC32 V2.1 candidate) (`CANDIDATE / TBD`).
- **Alternative Candidate:** 8-bit / 32-bit Arduino CNC Shield / STM32 controller (`TBD`).
- **Firmware Candidate:** FluidNC / grblHAL open-source motion control firmware (`CANDIDATE / TBD`).
- **Communication Interface:** USB Serial / Wi-Fi WebUI / Bluetooth G-code streaming.

### 3.2 Stepper Motor Drivers
- **Candidate Drivers:** Trinamic TMC2208 or TMC2209 silent stepper motor drivers (`CANDIDATE / TBD`).
- **Drive Mode:** StealthChop2 / SpreadCycle for silent, vibration-free pen carriage translation.
- **Current Tuning Target:** $V_{ref}$ adjusted to deliver $0.8\text{ A} - 1.0\text{ A}$ RMS to NEMA 17 coils (`PROPOSED RANGE / TBD`).

### 3.3 Z-Lifter Servo Power Isolation
- **Regulator Architecture:** Dedicated DC-DC step-down buck converter (e.g. LM2596 / MP1584 module) providing $5.0\text{V} \pm 0.1\text{V}$, minimum $2.0\text{A}$ continuous output (`CANDIDATE / TBD`).
- **Rationale:** Prevents servo inductive back-EMF and stall-current transients ($> 800\text{ mA}$) from causing 3.3V/5V microcontroller brownouts.

### 3.4 Power Supply Unit (PSU)
- **Input Voltage:** $100 - 240\text{V AC}, 50/60\text{ Hz}$.
- **Output Voltage:** $24.0\text{V DC}$ (or $12.0\text{V DC}$) regulated switching power supply (`TBD`).
- **Rated Power:** $60\text{W} - 100\text{W}$ ($24\text{V} / 2.5\text{A} - 4\text{A}$) (`PROPOSED TARGET / TBD`).

### 3.5 Endstops & Homing Sensors
- **X-Axis Minimum ($X_{min}$):** Microswitch (Normally Closed / NC loop for fail-safe wire break detection) located at gantry left corner.
- **Y-Axis Minimum ($Y_{min}$):** Microswitch (NC loop) located at frame front-left corner.
- **Dock Proximity / Optical Sensor:** Optional infrared reflective sensor at dock slots for tool occupancy confirmation (`CONCEPT / TBD`).

## 5. Wiring & Cable Management Guidelines

1. **Gantry Cable Chain / Umbilical:** Lightweight flexible drag chain or braided wire harness connecting the main electronics enclosure to the moving X-carriage.
2. **Signal Separation:** Route low-voltage servo PWM and endstop signal lines physically separated from high-current pulsed stepper motor phase wires to eliminate cross-talk.
3. **Emergency Stop:** Master DC power switch accessible on front chassis faceplate.
